Data quality

Multicollinearity is structural here: spectral indices share bands by construction, so NDVI and SAVI carry nearly the same information. A variance inflation factor above 10 means a coefficient on that predictor is not interpretable on its own.

04

Model fit, honestly measured

Two cross-validation designs run on the same model. Random folds let a test pixel sit metres from its training twin, which measures interpolation rather than generalisation. Spatial blocks withhold whole regions. The gap between them is how much of the apparent skill was really autocorrelation.

What drives the heat

Permutation importance: each predictor is shuffled in turn and the loss in accuracy recorded. Direction comes from the sign of its partial relationship with LST. This is model-agnostic and does not assume linearity.
